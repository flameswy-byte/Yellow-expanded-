Paint by dragging — mouse, touch or pen. Alt or the Pick tool samples a block. Neighbors render dimmed so seams line up. Collision shows walkable tiles in green. Buffer must stay at or under 1300: (w+6)×(h+6). On a phone use Copy .blk and paste the text back into chat; Export .blk downloads the raw file for desktop.
